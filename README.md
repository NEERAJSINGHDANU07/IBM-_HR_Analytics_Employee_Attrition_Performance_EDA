## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most significant challenges organizations face because replacing experienced employees can be expensive and time-consuming.

This project performs a comprehensive **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset** to identify the key factors associated with employee turnover.

The analysis transforms raw HR data into meaningful insights using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib**, helping understand employee demographics, job characteristics, compensation, satisfaction levels, work-life balance, and attrition patterns.

# 💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights several important patterns:

* Employees working overtime tend to experience higher attrition.
* Lower job satisfaction is associated with higher employee turnover.
* Monthly income varies significantly across different job roles.
* Employees with fewer years at the company generally show higher turnover.
* Work-life balance appears to influence employee retention.
* Certain departments and job roles experience relatively higher attrition rates.
* Age and total working experience also contribute to employee retention patterns.

> **Note:** These findings represent patterns observed within the analyzed dataset and should be interpreted as associations rather than direct causal relationships.
